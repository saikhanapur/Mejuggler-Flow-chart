<product_requirements>
SuperHumanly aims to transform complex Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) into interactive, AI-generated flowcharts for enterprise and SME clients. The core problem is to provide an intelligent tool that can identify and rectify broken business processes. Key requirements include:
1.  **Intelligent Grouping & Structure**: Generating flowcharts with 8-13 nodes per view, capable of intelligent grouping, accurate decision logic visualization, detection of parallel processes, monitoring loops, and handling multi-branch decision trees.
2.  **Detailed Operational Context**: Displaying operational details like contacts, systems, and timelines in interactive side panels.
3.  **AI Transparency**: Ensuring transparency in AI processing and decision-making.
4.  **Scalability**: Handling documents with 20+ steps and up to 50 pages without truncation.
5.  **Simplification for Quick Views**: Offering simplified views for quick comprehension.
6.  **EROAD-style Side Panel**: Providing a valuable, structured EROAD-style side panel.
7.  **Pixel-Perfect Design & Interactivity**: A consistently emphasized goal is to achieve pixel-perfect design and interactivity, precisely matching a user-provided HTML reference. This includes a clean canvas, compact nodes, specific vertical/L-shaped connection lines, prominent progress badges, and structured quick reference panels.
8.  **Multi-Process Document Handling**: The system must be able to detect and offer users options for handling documents containing multiple distinct processes.
9.  **Advanced AI Intelligence**: Enhancing AI to understand BCP-specific patterns, such as swim lane structures, BCP-specific decision points, time-based monitoring loops, and parallel activities by team.
10. **Version Control**: A basic versioning system is required, including creating snapshots upon process publishing and a simple version history UI.
</product_requirements>
