<product_requirements>
SuperHumanly aims to transform complex workflows into interactive, AI-generated flowcharts, targeting enterprise and SME customers. It ingests processes via voice, documents, and chat, utilizing the Claude API and featuring an Apple-inspired UI. Key features include multi-process detection, token-based sharing, Workspaces (now Projects), manual node editing, enterprise authentication, and legal pages.

The core problem SuperHumanly solves is evolving from a mere flowchart creator to a platform that identifies, quantifies, and recommends fixes for broken business processes. The goal is to provide measurable ROI and become indispensable in enterprise workflows. This involves node-specific issue detection, detailed explanations, and clear AI recommendations, initially targeting TIER 1 issues (missing error handling, bottlenecks, unclear ownership, missing timeouts, missing handoff documentation).

Recent focus has been on:
1.  **Guest Mode:** Allowing users to create one flowchart without signing up, while gating saving and advanced features, to reduce friction and drive product-led growth.
2.  **Detailed Process Capture:** Ensuring the AI extracts and presents all granular operational details (e.g., contact info, specific actions, required data fields) from source documents, not just high-level summaries.
3.  **Accurate Decision Logic Visualization:** Correctly representing decision points (e.g., diamonds) and clear branching paths (YES/NO) in flowcharts to enhance clarity and actionable insights.
4.  **Customer Confidence:** Developing mechanisms (like a Smart Extraction Preview and AI Coverage Report) to assure users that the AI accurately captures all essential information from their documents.
</product_requirements>
