<product_requirements>
SuperHumanly transforms complex Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) into interactive, AI-generated flowcharts, targeting enterprise/SME customers. The core problem is intelligently identifying, quantifying, and rectifying broken business processes from complex documents.

Key requirements for an enterprise-grade solution include:
1.  **Intelligent Grouping**: Logically group steps into strategic phases (e.g., 8-13 nodes), preventing overwhelming detail.
2.  **Accurate Decision Logic**: Clearly visualize decision points and branching paths.
3.  **Detailed Operational Capture**: Extract granular operational details to enrich a side panel with actionable information (contacts, systems, timelines, communication templates).
4.  **Transparency & Collaboration**: Show AI processing steps, identify document sections, and enable user clarification.
5.  **Scalability**: Robustly handle documents with 20+ steps without oversimplification or parsing errors.
6.  **Simplification**: Simplify complex documents into quick, digestible views (e.g., 5-7 critical steps) while retaining detailed views.
7.  **Valuable Side Panel**: Provide rich, purpose-driven context mirroring the EROAD example.
8.  **Pixel-Perfect Design**: The ultimate goal is to match the visual design and interactivity of a provided HTML reference (https://saikhanapur.github.io/Complex-SOP/) exactly, featuring a clean canvas, compact nodes, specific connection lines (vertical/L-shaped), progress badges, and structured quick reference panels.
</product_requirements>
