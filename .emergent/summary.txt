<product_requirements>
SuperHumanly aims to convert complex workflows into interactive, AI-generated flowcharts for enterprise/SME customers. It ingests processes via various inputs, using Claude API with an Apple-inspired UI. The core problem evolved from simple flowchart creation to identifying, quantifying, and fixing broken business processes, providing measurable ROI.

The current challenge centers on the AI's inability to handle complex, enterprise-level SOPs effectively. Key requirements include:
1.  **Intelligent Grouping**: Flowcharts should logically group steps into strategic phases, avoiding overwhelming detail.
2.  **Accurate Decision Logic**: Visually represent decision points (diamonds) and branching paths (YES/NO) clearly.
3.  **Detailed Operational Capture**: Extract granular operational details beyond high-level summaries, enriching the side panel with actionable information (contacts, systems, timelines, communication templates).
4.  **Transparency & Collaboration**: Show AI processing steps (not a black box), identify document sections (process, context, references), and engage users for clarification on complex documents.
5.  **Scalability**: Robustly handle documents with 20+ steps without oversimplification or parsing errors.
</product_requirements>
